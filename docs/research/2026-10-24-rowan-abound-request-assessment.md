## Assessment of the attached analysis

Verdicts below concern the argument or arithmetic where stated. They do not certify source counts.

| Claim                                                                                         | Initial verdict                                  | Reason and verification needed                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| People and households are different measures                                                  | Supported methodologically                       | Both are useful when accurately labelled. A recorded donor is not a complete measure of adult participation where couples give jointly.                                                                                  |
| Central carries 59% of adults                                                                 | Contradicted by the report's own arithmetic      | Its table shows 618 of 1,146 adults, or 53.9%. Central's 153 of 242 September giving households is 63.2%. The underlying counts still need reproduction.                                                                 |
| 463 / 1,146 adults is approximately 40%                                                       | Arithmetic verified                              | 40.4%. Production population, time window, filters and fund scope remain unverified.                                                                                                                                     |
| 242 / approximately 730 households is approximately 33%                                       | Arithmetic verified with provisional denominator | 33.2%. Removing 44 from 776 gives 732, rounded to about 730, but exclusions require identity and membership review.                                                                                                      |
| A 1.4 people-to-households ratio establishes about 130 two-giver and 205 one-giver households | Unsupported inference                            | Requires a matched household distribution and assumes at most two recorded givers. Neither follows from the two totals alone.                                                                                            |
| UniChurch's annual-people/monthly-households ratio reflects student flats                     | Unsupported explanation                          | Mismatched periods cannot establish household composition. The report separately describes most UniChurch households as single students. Inspect actual memberships and aligned windows.                                 |
| Xero and Abound agree once windows are aligned                                                | Unresolved                                       | Their grouping, fund scope and windows differ. A monthly total lying between annual and regular-giver totals is plausible, but is not reconciliation. Obtain the source analysis/export and match units and definitions. |
| About 600 adults have never given                                                             | Unresolved estimate                              | Neither 683 adults without a recent gift nor the all-record lifecycle card identifies the adult never-given population. Recompute the precise cohort.                                                                    |
| 44 records are not households and about 45 are duplicates                                     | Unresolved record judgments                      | Names can flag review candidates, but do not establish invalidity or identity. Keep a review list and record staff decisions in Rock.                                                                                    |
| Same-campus name pairs necessarily cause duplicate emails                                     | Unresolved                                       | Duplicate source identities may affect audiences, but actual send behaviour depends on recipient selection and suppression. Check existing workflows before asserting duplicate delivery.                                |
| 44 at-risk households / 242 September giving households is an 18% risk rate                   | Denominator unresolved                           | The division is 18.2%, but verify that the chart's at-risk cohort is a subset of September donors before presenting it as that rate.                                                                                     |

The proposed public headline should wait for cohort and definition verification. The 8 November cleanup action and 29 November campaign reference come from the source report; neither is an agreed delivery commitment in this work.
