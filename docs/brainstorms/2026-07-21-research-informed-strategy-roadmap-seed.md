# Research-Informed Strategy

## Problem Frame

Staff want to incorporate credible third-party research on church giving, generosity, donor development, and communication strategy. Research should guide ideas and recommendations without replacing staff judgment or creating automatic communication behavior.

## Seed Requirements

- R1. Staff should be able to reference curated third-party research about church giving, generosity, donor development, and communication strategy.
- R2. Research-informed recommendations should cite or name the source behind the idea.
- R3. Research should guide strategy and message ideas without replacing staff judgment.
- R4. Research-backed recommendations should remain compatible with Abound's sensitive-data and human-review boundaries.
- R5. Staff should be able to connect research themes to campaigns, journey ideas, opportunity insights, or content templates.

## Scope Boundaries

- Do not choose research sources without a dedicated research pass.
- Do not treat research-backed suggestions as automatic instructions to message donors.
- Do not mix research-backed suggestions with local Abound data insights without labeling the difference.

## Questions To Pick Up

- Which third-party research sources are credible and appropriate for this church context?
- Should research be stored as curated internal notes, source links, summarized findings, or structured recommendation rules?
- How should staff distinguish research-backed suggestions from local Abound data insights?
